# Artwork provenance and prompt brief

Raster artwork was created with the built-in image-generation tool. The game consumes the WebP copies under `public/skyline-estates/redesign/art/`. The original generated images are retained outside the public build.

City prompt template: “Original premium Skyline Estates game environment illustration of [CITY, COUNTRY]: [LOCAL ARCHITECTURAL / LANDSCAPE FEATURES]. Detailed stylized miniature 3D architectural diorama, warm limestone, petrol navy, muted emerald, terracotta and subtle satin brass accents. Soft daylight, collectible city aesthetic, wide 16:9 composition. No UI, words, letters, logos or playing board. Original illustrative artwork, not a geographic map.”

- Harbour: miniature harbour at blue hour, illuminated waterfront homes, palms, mountains, small lighthouse on the right, dark left area for UI copy; navy/emerald/limestone/brass.
- Starter bundle: original gold Art Deco skyline token, embossed skyline coins and navy dice on a limestone plinth; no lettering or UI.
- Modes: one display of three golden sculptural groups—people, globe and sliders—on a limestone garden plinth, navy background; no text.
- Wheel: original navy, emerald and brass prize wheel with coin/star/house/lightning symbols, limestone garden plinth, wheel on the right and dark left area; no lettering.

Rendered interface text, balances, progress, item prices and buttons are native HTML, not part of generated images. Product and token previews use the real game meshes; city illustrations do not claim exact geography.
